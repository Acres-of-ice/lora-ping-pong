What the colours mean
Solid white, full brightnessMax drain is running. White lights all three dies at full current: the most power the LED can draw.
White, blinking twice a secondMax drain is on and will start when the boot delay ends.
Blue tickA packet went out.
Green / yellow-green / amber / orange flashAcknowledged. The colour is the SNR margin of the weaker direction: 10 dB or more / 5–10 / 0–5 / below 0.
Long red flashNo acknowledgement came back.
Red double flashThe radio refused to transmit.
Violet double flashCarried out a command from the receiver's dashboard.
Cyan triple flashA new radio profile went live (provisional).
Red, then cyanThe provisional profile carried nothing and was rolled back.
Green, then cyanThe provisional profile proved itself and was saved.
Red blink every 2 sLink down: three exchanges in a row went unanswered.
Cyan double blink every 2 sThe radio profile is still provisional.
Orange triple blink every 4 sBattery below – V.
Amber blink every 3 sStopped; still checking in with the receiver.
Green blip every 5 sIdle and healthy. Only shows when nothing else has lit the LED for 5 s.
Red, blinking 5 times a secondThe radio failed to start: wiring or 3V3 to the module.
